========================================================================
  A. ELIMINATED AI PATTERNS
========================================================================

1. Formulaic section openers removed:
   BEFORE: "The remainder of the paper proceeds as follows."
   AFTER:  Direct launch into section roadmap.

2. Overused transitions replaced:
   "Furthermore" / "Moreover" -> Natural flow or varied connectors
   "However," sentence starts -> "That said," / em-dash / restructured
   "In addition" -> eliminated (information is inherently additive)

3. Robotically parallel structures broken:
   BEFORE: "We present... We derive... We develop... We show..."
   AFTER:  Varied: "This paper introduces..." / passive / gerund openings

4. AI hedging patterns reduced:
   "It is worth noting that" -> deleted or integrated
   "We emphasize that" -> reduced frequency
   "merit consideration" -> "merit consideration" (kept when appropriate)
   "deserve emphasis" -> restructured

5. Pompous academic formulations simplified:
   "a manifestation of a well-known phenomenon" -> "a well-known phenomenon"
   "None of these difficulties yield to a single technique alone"
     -> "No single technique addresses all three"
   "is one of Bayesian inference" -> "is Bayesian inference"

========================================================================
  B. LANGUAGE POLISHING HIGHLIGHTS
========================================================================

ABSTRACT:
  "Here we introduce" -> "This paper introduces" (formal register)
  "integrates... with" -> "couples... with" (precise verb)
  "develop" -> "propose" / "devise" (lexical variety)
  "degrades gracefully" -> "a graceful rather than catastrophic degradation"
  "are released to facilitate reproducibility" -> "are released" (less AI)

INTRODUCTION:
  "principally" -> "chiefly"
  "offer greater expressivity" -> "bring greater expressivity"
  "remarkable accuracy" -> "striking accuracy"
  "introduces challenges" -> "raises fresh challenges"
  "is the central task of this paper" kept as strong closer

DISCUSSION:
  "are worth understanding separately" -> "Understanding each
   separately is instructive" (more natural)
  "is influenced by the likelihood gradient" ->
   "respond to the likelihood gradient" (active voice)
  "edges ahead due to" -> "edges ahead through" (precise preposition)
  "a Kalman filter at heart" -> "a Kalman filter at its core"
